# Code of conduct

Sparkience AI is committed to a respectful, safe, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and professional.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and project boundaries.
- Accept maintainer decisions and disagree without harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, plagiarism, and disclosure of another person's private information are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainer identified in `CODEOWNERS`. For a concern requiring privacy, follow the private-contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and state that the report concerns community conduct rather than a vulnerability.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently exclude participants. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-affiliated community spaces.
